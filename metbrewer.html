R package

{MetBrewer}

Color palettes based on works in the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York. The source pieces span a range of time periods, regions, and mediums.

  • 56 palettes
  • 24 colorblind friendly
  • On CRAN

{MetBrewer}'s structure is based on code from {PNWColors} and {wesanderson}, and the project as a whole is indebted to {RColorBrewer} and Cynthia Brewer's work. Colorblind friendliness was checked with {colorblindcheck}.

Install it:

install.packages("MetBrewer")
