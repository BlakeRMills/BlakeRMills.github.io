The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York. Accession number 23.128.
Object 437798 — https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437798

Public domain, released under the Met's Open Access programme (CC0 1.0).

This file is a crop of the painted surface — the scan includes the gilt
cartouche frame on black, which is cropped out — resized to 2200px wide
and encoded as WebP at q78.
