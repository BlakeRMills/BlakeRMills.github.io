Visualizations

A collection of some of my favorite data visualizations. All of them were made in R, mostly with {ggplot2}, {tidyverse}, and the usual spatial suspects.

#TidyTuesday City Maps by Streets Staying on my Street — Costs of Airbnbs City Streets by Elevation HOLC Redlining & Police Killings Other Entries

Code for the weekly plots lives in the TidyTuesday repo, and the maps in the 30DayMapChallenge repo. The #30DayMapChallenge was started by Topi Tjukanov — one themed map a day, every November.

#TidyTuesday

A weekly challenge from the R for Data Science community: a new dataset every week, and whatever you can make of it.

City Maps by Streets

Every street in a city, drawn by type. Day one of the challenge turned into a series.

Staying on my Street — Costs of Airbnbs

What it costs to stay on each street, mapped block by block.

City Streets by Elevation

The same street networks, colored by how high above sea level they sit.

HOLC Redlining & Police Killings

1930s HOLC redlining grades laid against the locations of police killings.

Other Entries
